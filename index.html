How to Play

  • Online: one player taps Create Room and shares the 5‑digit room number. The other taps Join Room and types it in.
  • Each fighter has 3 lives. Lose a life when your HP hits 0 or you get knocked off the stage. Last stickman standing wins!
  • The lower your HP, the further you fly when hit.
  • Left side: drag to move. Flick up to jump, hold down to drop through thin platforms.
  • ATK – normal attack (hold to keep attacking). SKILL – your weapon's special move. JUMP – tap twice to double jump. GRAB – pick up a weapon that dropped from the sky.
  • Every one of the 50 weapons has its own skill: dashes, lightning, black holes, freezing, teleports, meteors, shields and more.
  • Keyboard: A/D move · W/Space jump · S drop · J attack · K skill · L grab.
  • Install: in Chrome tap ⋮ → Install app / Add to Home screen to play it like a normal app.
